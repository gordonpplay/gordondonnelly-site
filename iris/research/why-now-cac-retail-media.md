# Why Now: ad cost inflation and retail media (research pack, 2026-10-13)

Source: research agent supplement, Section 3 of the WHY NOW report. Kept in full because the
deck cites three lines from it and the rest is the backing. Labels are the agent's:
MEASURED = primary or company-reported; DERIVED = our arithmetic; ESTIMATED = forecast or
agency-reported; UNVERIFIED = do not cite.

## Meta average price per ad (MEASURED, Meta quarterly earnings releases)

| Period | Price per ad YoY | Impressions YoY |
|---|---|---|
| Q2 2023 | -16% | +34% |
| Q4 2024 | +14% | +6% |
| FY 2024 | +10% | +11% |
| Q3 2025 | +10% | +14% |
| Q4 2025 | +6% | +18% |
| FY 2025 | +9% | +12% |
| Q1 2026 | +12% | +19% |
| Q2 2026 | +12% | +14% |

Verbatim, Q2 2026 (29 Jul 2026): "Ad impressions delivered across our Family of Apps increased
by 14% year-over-year." / "Average price per ad increased by 12% year-over-year."
Verbatim, Q4/FY2025 (28 Jan 2026): "Average price per ad increased by 6% and 9% year-over-year
for the fourth quarter and full year 2025, respectively."

DERIVED: chaining FY2024 +10%, FY2025 +9%, 2026 H1 +12% gives roughly +33% to +35% cumulative
from end-2023 to mid-2026, off a 2023 trough where prices had FALLEN 16%.

Structural point: impressions still growing 14% to 19% WHILE price is up 12%. Supply expansion
no longer holds price down. That is what broke the 2022-23 playbook.

IR URL pattern: investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/<year>/Meta-Reports-...

## Google and Microsoft search CPC

MEASURED, LocaliQ/WordStream Search Advertising Benchmarks, updated 1 Jun 2026
(localiq.com/blog/search-advertising-benchmarks/). Methodology: "thousands of our customers'
advertising campaigns across Google Ads and Microsoft Ads". Account count and date range not
disclosed, a real limitation.
- Google Ads CPC $5.42 all industries; CPL $66.69; conversion rate 8.18%
- Apparel/Fashion/Jewelry $4.44; Shopping/Collectibles/Gifts $4.14; Furniture $3.97
- Report notes "Cost per lead decreased overall for the first time in five years"

Secondhand, via PPC Land, 10 Oct 2026 ("Google optimises its own revenue..."):
- $5.42 median CPC 2026 vs $2.32 in 2016, +134% over the decade (WordStream 2026). MEASURED.
- CPC +12.88% to $5.26 (LocaliQ 2025). MEASURED, secondhand.
- Google Search CPC +13% YoY while average order values rose about 1% (Tinuiti, Q1 2024).
  MEASURED, secondhand. Best single stat for a margin argument: click cost and order value
  came apart.
- Microsoft Advertising CPC +19% YoY Q2 2026. MEASURED, secondhand.
- Google Search CPC +1% in Q2 2026 (Tinuiti via MediaPost, Jul 2026). HONESTY CHECK: Google
  text-search CPC inflation COOLED in 2026. The 2026 pressure is Meta, Amazon, Shopping/PMax
  and retail media, not Google text search. Do not overclaim Google.
- AI Max drove 10-15% average CPC increases for Mediaplus clients, some as high as 25%; about
  10% average across Collective Measures clients (Digiday, Aug 2025). ESTIMATED, agency-reported.
- Alphabet Google Search and other revenue Q2 2026: $63.27bn, +17% YoY. MEASURED, secondhand.

## Amazon advertising

Ad cost change Q1 2019 to Q1 2025. MEASURED secondhand: Andy Taylor, head of research at
Tinuiti, via Modern Retail 8 Apr 2025:
- Amazon Sponsored Products +48%
- Google paid search +41%, Instagram +37%, Facebook -24%

Cleanest available statement that Amazon is now the fastest-inflating of the big three for a
physical-product seller.

Amazon advertising services net sales, MEASURED, official releases
(ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/...):
- Q3 2025: $17.703bn vs $14.331bn, +24%
- Q4 2025: $21.317bn vs $17.288bn, +23% (+22% FX-neutral)
- Q1 2026: $17.243bn vs $13.921bn, +24%
- Q2 2026: $19.809bn vs $15.694bn, +26%
- FY2025 $68.635bn (DERIVED, sum of four official quarters). Amazon characterised the year as
  "Advertising growing 22%."
- Andy Jassy, Q1 2026: "Advertising grew to over $70 billion in TTM revenue."
- Andy Jassy, Q2 2026: "Advertising had another strong quarter with 26% year-over-year growth."
- 2024 baseline $56bn, +18% YoY. MEASURED secondhand via Modern Retail.
- ESTIMATED: EMARKETER forecast $85bn for 2026.
- Context Q2 2026: Amazon total net sales $200.6bn (+20%), operating income $27.5bn (+43%).

Why-now is the ACCELERATION: ad revenue growth went +23% to +26% across Q4 2025 to Q2 2026
while the brands funding it absorbed the Jan 2026 fee increase and the Apr 2026 fuel surcharge
in the same window.

NOT FOUND: no publicly available Amazon Sponsored Products CPC figure for 2025 or 2026.
Tinuiti's benchmark report is lead-gated, Skai's is gated, Pacvue returns 403. Do not assume
a 2026 Amazon CPC number.

## Retail media networks

WALMART, MEASURED company-reported via Modern Retail 22 Aug 2025:
- Global ad revenue +46% YoY; Walmart Connect US +31% excluding Vizio
- About HALF of Walmart's incremental profit in the quarter came from advertising, membership
  and marketplace fees
- Baseline (Modern Retail 9 Sep 2025): $4.4bn in 2024, +27% YoY; ads now nearly one-third of
  Walmart's total profit
- "From a business model point of view, the fact that we have businesses like advertising and
  membership growing obviously helps with flexibility as it relates to when we decide to absorb
  part of a tariff cost increase." Doug McMillon, President and CEO, Walmart
- "Walmart's global advertising growth is outpacing the U.S. The company's marketplace expansion
  is fueling this, as more sellers and assortment create new advertising opportunities."
  Sarah Marzano, Principal Analyst, EMARKETER

TARGET ROUNDEL, MEASURED practitioner survey, Modern Retail+ Research, 26 Aug 2026:
- Target Roundel budgets DOUBLED in 2026 vs 2025 on a weighted-average basis among surveyed
  marketers. Single most direct retail-media escalation number in the pack.
- RMN usage 2026: Amazon 89%, Walmart Connect 47%, Target Roundel 32%
- "Cost of media" named by 33.33% of respondents as a top challenge on The Home Depot's Orange
  Apron Media
- Modern Retail 4 Mar 2026: 35% of Target's retail media revenue is off-site; Target 2025
  operating margin 4.6%, guided up about 20bps in 2026, vs 6% pre-pandemic
- "Amazon is the must-play and is usually a reflection of our growth and spend as a percent of
  sales." Jeremy Lowenstein, CMO, Milani Cosmetics

KROGER PRECISION MARKETING, MEASURED company-reported via Modern Retail 11 Sep 2026:
- Fiscal Q2 2026: KPM advertising profit +24%, strongest profit growth since 2021
- Media monetization up 88 basis points YoY
- "Stronger collaboration between our merchandising and media teams expanded advertising
  inventory, and optimization efforts improved visibility and conversion for our brand
  partners." Greg Foran, CEO, Kroger. Read plainly: more ad inventory to sell to suppliers.

RETAIL MEDIA AS A TAX ON EXISTING BUDGET. Modern Retail, "'The easy dollars are gone': Retail
media faces new tests as it nears maturity," 9 Sep 2025:
- "the easy dollars are gone" Derek Nelson, Ovative Group
- "Well, what are we doing here? These are huge pots of money that we're not playing in"
  Derek Nelson, on board-level pressure to fund retail media
- retail media spend "is not like, all of a sudden, you just have 10% more to spend... You have
  to take 10% from elsewhere" Nick Drabicky, January Digital
- retail media "has gone from an emerging media channel to a major media channel, and with that
  maturity... we're going to see the rates of growth start to decelerate" Sarah Marzano, EMARKETER
- IAB commerce ad spend growth 15.6% expected 2025 vs 25.1% in 2024 (MEASURED, industry body);
  EMARKETER projects retail media growth 17.5% in 2025 decelerating to 10.4% by 2029 (ESTIMATED)

WALMART CONNECT ONLY ADDED NEGATIVE KEYWORDS IN AUGUST 2026, so brands were paying for
non-incremental and irrelevant terms until then. Modern Retail 11 Aug 2026:
- "Not having negative key terms meant that we were spending on key terms that were not giving
  us return." Ray Easley, Senior Director of Commerce, Flywheel
- "In a world that's obsessed with incrementality, the least incremental form of advertisement
  is your branded terms." / "Your account [return on ad spend] is also going to go down."
  Chris Sheldon, Consulting Leader, Podean

SLOTTING, TRADE AND CO-OP FEE INFLATION: NOT FINDABLE as a number. Closest, qualitatively:

## Operator-level CAC deterioration (best material in the section)

MEASURED, brand-reported. Modern Retail 2 Jun 2025, "Why candle brand Siblings dropped its Meta
spend by 56%":
- CAC went $25 in 2022 to $60 in 2025, +140%
- CPM rose $15 in 2024 with NO change to the ads
- Meta spend cut 56% over the year; LTV up 30% in 2024
- "That math just doesn't work anymore." David Bronkie, co-founder, Siblings, on a $60 CAC
- "We had strong consistency on Meta through all of '21, '22, '23. But we hit a wall at the
  beginning of last year, where we saw a $15 jump in our CPMs with no change to anything in our
  ads." David Bronkie
- "There is so much we can be doing that doesn't rely on paying 'the Meta tax.'" David Bronkie
- "It's not so much of an ROI issue for brands right now. It's more of a general instability
  because, over the past year, Meta has been spending a ton of money on AI." Danavir Sarria,
  founder, SupplyDrop

Corroborating:
- 25% to 40% rise in customer acquisition costs across Meta, YouTube and podcast advertising.
  Mike Salguero, founder and CEO, ButcherBox, Modern Retail 21 May 2025. MEASURED,
  brand-reported. "Direct-to-consumer is having a really tough time right now."
- "Digital CAC costs are much higher and customers are consuming content in different ways." /
  "You used to have five or six years before copycats would come to market. That happens in six
  months now." Jessica Schinazi, CEO, Away, Modern Retail 13 Jan 2026. No number given.
- 2023 baseline, Modern Retail 17 Jun 2024: Facebook CPM $14, Google CPM $10.50 in 2023.

NOT FOUND: no public measured blended-CAC or MER panel benchmark for 2025 or 2026. Common

## The three strongest items (agent's ranking, and what the deck cites)

1. Meta average price per ad swung from -16% YoY in Q2 2023 to +12% YoY in BOTH Q1 and Q2 2026,
   while impressions also grew 14% to 19%. Official earnings releases, filing-grade. Roughly
   +33% to +35% cumulative since end-2023 (derived). The old lever, buy more impressions to hold
   cost down, no longer works. **CITED ON SLIDE 7.**
2. Amazon Sponsored Products ad costs +48% from Q1 2019 to Q1 2025 versus Facebook -24% over the
   same window, and Amazon ad revenue growth ACCELERATING to +26% YoY in Q2 2026 on $68.6bn of
   FY2025 advertising. The marketplace channel is now the expensive one. **CITED ON SLIDE 7.**
3. Retail media is reallocation, not new money, and it is scaling fast. Target Roundel budgets
   DOUBLED 2026 vs 2025 among surveyed marketers; Kroger KPM profit +24% in fiscal Q2 2026;
   Walmart ads +46% globally with advertising, membership and marketplace fees supplying half of
   incremental profit. Set against Drabicky: "You have to take 10% from elsewhere."
   **CITED ON SLIDE 7.**

## Tooling notes

WordPress `?s=` search works on modernretail.co, digiday.com and tinuiti.com. Meta's and
Amazon's investor-relations `press-release-details` and `news-release-details` URL patterns are
reliable and return filing-grade numbers in a single fetch, better than SEC 10-Q htm files for
revenue lines. The Federal Register API and EDGAR full-text search both work and need no search
budget. Bing via WebFetch returns unrelated results most of the time, abandon it. DuckDuckGo is
captcha-blocked, Google is blocked, Mojeek returns 403.
