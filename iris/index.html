Who we do not sell to. Under $5M, where the pain is real and there is no budget to fix it. And over $150M, where you are displacing an instrumented NetSuite and a staffed FP&A team that has already solved this.
Where I have done this. At Knock2 I defined the ICP and then owned everything downstream of it. The company-level ICP was B2B SaaS and services companies with enough inbound traffic for visitor identification to pay back, and inside it we targeted four buying roles: RevOps leads, demand gen leads, VPs of Sales and VPs of Marketing. These personas informed every motion we ran: the cold outbound sequences, the persona and use-case pages on the site, and paid on LinkedIn, Google and Meta, with outbound and advertising deliberately working the same accounts in the same week.

How it runs

Free toolsWhat Amazon actually takes, price point and category in, real take rate out. True landed cost, every input in, real COGS per unit out.
